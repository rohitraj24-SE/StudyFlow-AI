📝 Quick Study Tips

⏱️

Use the Pomodoro rhythm

25 minutes focused, 5 minutes off. It's easier to start a short sprint than an open-ended session.

🎯

One goal per session

A vague "study math" plan gets abandoned. "Finish 10 integration problems" gets done.

🔁

Spaced repetition beats cramming

Short, frequent reviews across days beat one long session the night before.

🌙

Protect your sleep

Memory consolidation happens while you sleep — an all-nighter often undoes the studying itself.
